## MOOSE Bug Fixes

- Apps without a Console output (e.g. `[Outputs] console = false`) no longer keep every message
  written to `_console` in memory. The buffer was never cleared and was copied at every flush, so
  long-running (sub-)apps, such as a MultiApp running many solves in one process, became slower
  and slower with the number of messages.
  ([idaholab/moose#34118](https://github.com/idaholab/moose/issues/34118))
- Linear finite volume boundary conditions on an internal boundary now evaluate their functors
  on the side of the variable they act on, also when the functor is defined on both sides.
  Previously, a functor defined block by block was interpolated between the two blocks, so
  results change for such boundary conditions. `LinearFVAnisotropicDiffusionFunctorNeumannBC`
  now also evaluates a flux or diffusion tensor defined only on the other side of the boundary.

## MOOSE Modules Changes

### Contact module

Mechanical mortar contact now assembles the contact force by interpolating the nodal traction
vector with the traction basis, instead of scaling an interpolated scalar pressure by the nodal
normal of the residual row's own node. Only the former is the transpose of the weighted gap, so the
contact force is now self-equilibrated across the interface and no longer depends on secondary node
numbering. Contact forces and converged results therefore change on interfaces where the secondary
nodal normals vary along the face.

Local-basis quasistatic `mortar` and `mortar_penalty` contact Jacobians now also include
derivatives of the averaged secondary nodal normals and their tangent basis. These derivatives can
require a larger AD container on high-order or high-valence interfaces. Because the contact
coupling changes during the solve in most contact problems, such as when surfaces slide or come into
contact, set `Problem/use_hash_table_matrix_assembly = true` to accommodate the changing sparsity
pattern
([idaholab/moose#33390](https://github.com/idaholab/moose/pull/33390)).

### Navier-Stokes module

The Navier-Stokes linear finite-volume SIMPLE solver now supports porous media. The formulation
solves for superficial velocity, includes Darcy and Forchheimer resistance, and applies Bernoulli
and irreversible form-loss pressure jumps on internal baffles. Jump-aware pressure correction and
gradient reconstruction keep the pressure equation, face flux, and momentum predictor on one
interface jump, and the same coupling supports porous enthalpy transport. These objects are
available through manual input syntax; see
[idaholab/moose#31995](https://github.com/idaholab/moose/pull/31995).
