## MOOSE Modules Changes

### Contact module

Mechanical mortar contact now assembles the contact force by interpolating the nodal traction
vector with the traction basis, instead of scaling an interpolated scalar pressure by the nodal
normal of the residual row's own node. Only the former is the transpose of the weighted gap, so the
contact force is now self-equilibrated across the interface and no longer depends on secondary node
numbering. Contact forces and converged results therefore change on interfaces where the secondary
nodal normals vary along the face.

Local-basis quasistatic `mortar` and `mortar_penalty` contact Jacobians now also include
derivatives of the averaged secondary nodal normals and their tangent basis. These derivatives can
require a larger AD container on high-order or high-valence interfaces. Because the contact
coupling changes during the solve in most contact problems, such as when surfaces slide or come into
contact, set `Problem/use_hash_table_matrix_assembly = true` to accommodate the changing sparsity
pattern
([idaholab/moose#33390](https://github.com/idaholab/moose/pull/33390)).

The [Contact action](ContactAction.md) has a new `c_strategy = physical` option for mortar contact.
It derives the normal and tangential contact constants at each secondary node from the normal and
shear acoustic moduli of the contacting bodies' elasticity tensors and their element depths, and scales the contact Lagrange multipliers so
that their Jacobian rows and columns are of the same order as the elasticity block. Users no longer
need to tune `c_normal`, `c_tangential`, or the multiplier scaling for these problems.

### Heat transfer module

[GapFluxModelPressureDependentConduction](GapFluxModelPressureDependentConduction.md) now obtains a
contact pressure from mechanical mortar contact through the new `contact_pressure_uo` parameter,
which names the mortar contact user object (`lm_weightedgap_object_<contact block name>` when the
Contact action creates it). The contact Lagrange multiplier variable can store a scaled pressure, so
coupling it through `contact_pressure` is now an error. Inputs that coupled the normal contact
multiplier must replace `contact_pressure = <normal multiplier>` with
`contact_pressure_uo = <contact user object>`. `contact_pressure` still accepts an auxiliary variable
holding a prescribed pressure.
