## MOOSE Improvements

### Remeshing system

MOOSE now has a [Remeshing](syntax/Remeshing/index.md) system that replaces elements of the mesh
during a transient simulation and optionally moves the mesh between those replacements. The new
top-level `[Remeshing]` block holds [Criteria](syntax/Remeshing/Criteria/index.md) that decide when a
replacement happens, [Remeshers](syntax/Remeshing/Remeshers/index.md) that perform it, and
[Smoothers](syntax/Remeshing/Smoothers/index.md) that move the mesh in between, either as
arbitrary Lagrangian-Eulerian mesh motion or alongside the true displacements of a mechanics
problem. Three remeshers ship for `TRI3` meshes: [PatchDelaunayRemesher.md] retriangulates patches
of poor-quality or over-refined elements, [TriSplitRemesher.md] refines against a target element
size field with a red-green split, and [TriEdgeRemesher.md] refines and coarsens with edge splits,
collapses and swaps. The solution, including stateful material properties carried through
[ProjectedStatefulMaterialStorage](syntax/ProjectedStatefulMaterialStorage/index.md), is transferred
onto the new elements, and [RemeshCount.md] and [MaxPseudoDisplacement.md] report the activity of
the system.
([idaholab/moose#13610](https://github.com/idaholab/moose/issues/13610))
