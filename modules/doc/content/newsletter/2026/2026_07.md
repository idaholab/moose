## MOOSE Modules Changes

### Contact module

Supported local-basis quasistatic `mortar` and non-augmented `mortar_penalty` contact Jacobians now
include derivatives of the averaged secondary nodal normals and their tangent basis without changing
residual values. These derivatives can require a larger AD container on high-order or high-valence
interfaces. For moving distributed contact, `Problem/use_hash_table_matrix_assembly = true`
accommodates sparsity changes during the simulation.
